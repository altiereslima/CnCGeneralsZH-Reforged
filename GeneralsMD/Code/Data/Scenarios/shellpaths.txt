# Projectile flight paths, one launcher per lane, each shooting an unarmed target: tank shell
# (Crusader), lobbed shells (Inferno Cannon, Nuke Cannon), missiles (Scud Launcher, Tomahawk,
# Rocket Buggy), tank shells out of raised barrels (the four Fire Bases, the base one at about 105 and
# then 160; the demo battleship at 330; the USS Bender's own shell at 230) and tank shells on
# uneven ground (a Crusader up a slope at 100, a Scorpion down one at 75, an Overlord at a car 40
# away and 10 degrees below). The paths are
# only in the log with a temporary per-frame trace of each projectile's position and nose, a
# DEBUG_LOG in DumbProjectileBehavior::update and MissileAIUpdate::update.
#
#   generals.exe -headless -quickstart -noshellmap -observer -multiInstance -noFPSLimit
#     -randommap 1 2 small -autoskirmish 2 -takeover
#     -side 0 FactionAmerica -side 1 FactionGLA -seed 1 -maxframes 900
#     -scenario shellpaths -logPrefix shellpaths_
#
# Fire Base, barrel against the shell's first and last direction (2026-10-25), logged at launch from
# the path DumbProjectileBehavior::projectileFireAtObjectOrPosition built: its barrel stood 46 and 53
# degrees up while the shell flew the line, 9 to 12 degrees down, because StraightWhenClear never
# looked at the barrel. It now leaves at 44 and 51 and comes down at 66 to 71. The Air Force, Laser
# and Superweapon Fire Bases do the same (barrel 38 to 57, leave 1.4 under it). Every tank barrel
# reads -2.5 or 0, since the logic never tilts a hull on a slope, and every tank shell, the
# Overlord's at 10 degrees down included, still flies the line. The battleships' front turret is aimed 12 up (20 on the USS Bender)
# but its launch matrix read 12 down (20 down): its art faces backward, it has no launch bone, and the
# identity matrix was turned by the art angle anyway. The demo battleship's shell flew the line and
# the Bender's took EA's arc, out at 43. Both now leave along the barrel: the demo's at 11, in at 17,
# peaking 19 over the line at 330; the Bender's at 21, in at 76, its own arc 63 over the line at
# 230. Inferno and Nuke Cannon shells are unchanged to the tenth of a degree.
#
# A battleship's shell starts about 65 beyond the drawn muzzle: with no launch bone the logic launches
# from the ship's origin swung round the turret's pivot, 180 to 190 from the ship's centre.
#
# Sharpest nose turn in one frame, before and after straight tank shells, barrel-led lobs and the
# cruise missiles' single curve (2026-10-13): tank shell 13.7 to 1.9 and 7.2 to 0 over the line, Nuke
# Cannon 6.5 to 3.8, Inferno 5.4 to 5.5, Scud 6.0 to 4.6, Tomahawk 6.0 to 5.5, Rocket Buggy 3.3 both.
#

# battleships fly a parabola set by their weapon, and the numbers above for them are history;
# artillery.txt measures them now.
#
