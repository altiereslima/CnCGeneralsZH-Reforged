# Five frames of one angry mob crossing open ground and hitting a Ranger platoon, for looking at
# what the formation does rather than for watching.  Runs with -scenario mobopen, which puts the
# mobs at 700,550 and the Rangers at 1000,1150 on the small generated map and sends the mobs at
# them on frame 120.
#
#   generals.exe -borderless -quickstart -noshellmap -observer -multiInstance -noFPSLimit
#     -randommap 1 2 small -autoskirmish 2 -takeover
#     -side 0 FactionGLA -side 1 FactionAmerica -seed 1 -maxframes 1800
#     -scenario mobopen -cinema mobwatch -logPrefix mobopenshots_
#
# The camera rides the nexus, so the mob stays in frame whatever the march does with it and the
# five pictures are comparable to each other and to the same five off another build.  Zoom 1.4
# holds about 600 map units across a 1920 frame, which is the mob plus the ground it has lost its
# stragglers onto; 0.65 held 275 and the mob walked out of the sides of it.  Pitch -20 is steep
# enough to read who is standing where.  Seconds are logic frames over 30.
